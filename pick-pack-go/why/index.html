    Why it works this way

    I have ADHD. I built this app because no travel app understood executive function, and no ADHD app understood packing.
    These pages are what actually goes wrong when I pack, told back to me by hundreds of strangers who describe it the same way. Each one ends with what the app does about it — and what it doesn’t.
