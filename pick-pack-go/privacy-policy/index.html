Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated: September 2026

Pick, Pack, Go is a travel packing app that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What Pick, Pack, Go stores

Your trips, packing lists, templates, and preferences are saved locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data stays on your device unless you choose to export it — see "Exporting your data" below.

iCloud backup

If you use iCloud to back up your iPhone, your Pick, Pack, Go data is included in that backup automatically—this is a standard iOS feature managed by Apple, not by Pick, Pack, Go. You can control this in your iPhone's Settings under iCloud Backup.

Deleting your data

Everything is on your device, so all of it is yours to remove. Settings → Reset Options will delete every trip and packing list, or restore your libraries to their defaults. Deleting Pick, Pack, Go removes everything it holds — though if your iPhone is backed up to iCloud, a copy remains in that backup until the backup is replaced or removed.

Exporting your data

Settings → Reset Options lets you save everything in your library — trips, sections, kits, items, and templates — as a single file, so you can keep a copy before deleting your data. This only happens when you choose to do it. Once you share or save that file, where it goes afterward is up to you and outside Pick, Pack, Go's control.

No accounts or sign-in

Pick, Pack, Go does not require or offer user accounts, email collection, or any form of sign-in.

No analytics or tracking

Pick, Pack, Go does not use analytics services, advertising frameworks, crash reporting tools, or any third-party code that collects data.

No network access

Pick, Pack, Go does not connect to the internet. The app functions fully offline.

Children's privacy

Pick, Pack, Go does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Pick, Pack, Go changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version ships.
