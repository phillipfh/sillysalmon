Changelog

Every version, what changed, and why.

1.1.2 — October 2026

A fix for a freeze. Closing an Add Item row while its keyboard was still up, in a template or on a trip's lists, could freeze the app. Now the row closes, the keyboard goes down, and you can keep going. Picking an emoji for a template, a trip, a section or a kit gets the same fix.

1.1.1 — October 2026

Two fixes to the editors in your library. Opening an item while typing in a section or kit's name or description no longer sends you back to the top when you close it, and an item showing where else it lives no longer stands taller than its neighbors when it sits under an item with a note. The in-app answer to "What's a Template?" also explains that a template holds your real library items, not copies.

1.1 — October 2026

Your trips, your templates and your library edits all come through this update.

A few of the built-in library items change name or quantity as part of it, and if an older reset left you with duplicate copies of the built-in templates, the ones you never changed are tidied away.

Saving a trip as a template is one screen instead of two. Your custom sections join your Section Library when you save, and a name that's already taken is settled on the same screen rather than in an alert.

A template holds your real library items now, not a second copy of them. It keeps what that template wants for each one — its own note, quantity, Critical and charge reminder — and a row you've changed says so. Type a new item into a template and it joins your Item Library when you save.

Reset Libraries no longer crashes. Be clear on what it does, though: it clears all three libraries — the templates you've saved included — and builds them back from the defaults. Your trips are kept.

Export Library Data, under Settings → Reset Options, writes everything to a CSV first, templates included. It's a readable archive rather than a backup: the app can't import it back.

Templates no longer carry anything from the Plans tab — outfits, activities and notes belong to one trip. Copying a trip still brings its outfits.

An outfit planned for a day your trip doesn't have moves to Anytime rather than sitting on a day that isn't there.

Two sections can no longer share a name.

The app looks and feels different: SF Rounded unless you picked a font, phases that slide in, lists that crossfade, quantity numbers that roll, a sheet's ✓ answering as your finger lands, section colors that hold up in Dark Mode and with Increase Contrast, and a refined app icon.

Smaller things: ticked items leave together rather than one at a time; the keyboard follows your finger down; taking an item off a trip is Remove, never Delete, and changes nothing outside that trip; a to-do is only taken with a deleted item when nothing else still needs it; one ＋ Add Item picker; sections before kits, A–Z; and dress clothes that scale with the trip.

Nothing leaves your phone unless you export it yourself.

1.0 — September 2026

First release.

Pick, Pack, Go is a packing app whose lists start full instead of empty, because for an ADHD brain the blank page is the hard part.

Everything in 1.0 is on the roadmap page under NOW.
