    Past trips

    The archive that makes the next trip easier.

    The gist

    • Completed trips move to Past Trips and become read-only.
    • "Copy as New Trip" is the fastest way to plan again — you get last time's list exactly as you left it.
    • You have three days to change your mind. After that a completed trip's dates lock.
    • Search, filter and sort by type, weather, transport, season, length or name.
    • Getting there: the View Past Trips button at the bottom of the Home screen.

    Completing a trip

    ••• → Mark Trip Complete, from inside the trip. It moves to Past Trips and everything in it becomes read-only — no checking, no editing, no adding.

    If you forget, the app asks. Three days after your return date, opening Home brings up:

    Mark [trip name] complete? It's been 4 days since you returned from your trip. Would you like to mark this trip complete? (12 items remain unchecked)

    Mark Complete or Not Yet. Declining isn't permanent — it'll ask again in another few days.

    Reopening one

    Within three days of the return date, a completed trip can be reopened: press and hold it in Past Trips → Reopen Trip. It goes back to being an active trip.

    After that window it stays read-only, and its dates can no longer be edited. Its classification — type, weather, transport, season — stays editable forever, so you can keep the archive organised.

    Doing it again

    Press and hold any past trip:

    ActionWhat you get
    Copy as New TripA brand-new trip with that trip's exact list — everything you added, everything you deleted, every quantity you changed
    Save as TemplateTurns it into a reusable template, then opens the editor so you can tidy it
    Edit TripChange its details
    Reopen TripBack to active (within three days of return)
    Delete TripPermanent, with a confirmation

    Copy as New Trip is the underrated one. For "the same trip again", it beats templates: no maintenance, no separate thing to keep updated, and it preserves the refinements you made last time rather than the ones you imagined when you built a template.

    Finding one

    Search by name at the top.

    Filter by trip type, duration, transportation, weather and season. Active filters show as chips you can dismiss individually, with Clear All when several are on.

    Sort by:

    GroupOptions
    DepartureRecent First · Oldest First
    DurationShortest First · Longest First
    AlphabeticalA → Z · Z → A

    Go deeper

    Read a past trip like a record. Open it and you can walk its five phases exactly as they were — including a section that ended up empty, which is labelled "Section contained no items." That's a useful signal when you're deciding what to cut next time.

    The Return phase is what makes the archive worth having. What Worked Well, What Didn't Work, For Next Time. Two minutes of writing when you get home is what turns a pile of old trips into something that improves the next one.

    Completed trips show everything. Hide Completed is forced off in a completed trip, so you see the full list as it stood, ticks and all.

    If something looks wrong

    My trip isn't in Past Trips. It hasn't been marked complete. Open it and use ••• → Mark Trip Complete, or wait for the Home screen to ask.

    I can't find the Past Trips button. It only appears once you have at least one completed trip — at the bottom of the Home screen, or in the middle of the empty state if you have no active trips.

    I can't edit a past trip's dates. More than three days have passed since its return date. Type, weather, transport and season are still editable.

    I copied a trip and the quantities look wrong. A copy keeps the original's numbers rather than recalculating for new dates. If the new trip is a different length, adjust anything counted per-day. See Quantity formulas.

    I deleted a past trip by mistake. There's no undo. If you'd saved it as a template, that template is unaffected — templates are independent copies.
