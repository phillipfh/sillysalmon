    Sections and kits

    Where the 45 starting lists come from, and how to make them yours.

    The gist

    • Settings → Sections & Kits Library holds 21 ready-made lists. Browse Kits offers 15 more you can add — 36 in all.
    • A section files things; a kit is a bag you grab. Two kits can each own sunscreen. Two sections share one licence.
    • Priority decides what you're offered: Essential (pre-ticked), Favorite (offered high), No Priority (offered when relevant).
    • Tags decide when a section is relevant — trip type, weather, season, transport, length.
    • Archive instead of deleting. Archived lists stop being offered but can come back.

    What's in the box

    CountExamples
    Sections27Clothes, Toiletries, Personal Items, Travel Documents, Tech, Home, On You
    Kits18Camera Kit, Makeup Kit, Hiking Gear, Ski Gear, Beach Kit, Baby Gear, Golf Gear

    Between them they hold 863 distinct items.

    Eight are Essentials, pre-ticked when you make a trip: Clothes, Personal Items, Toiletries, Travel Documents, Plane Essentials, Car Essentials, Train Essentials, Travel Outfit. Only your transportation choice filters which of those you get — say you're flying and Car Essentials stays out of it.

    Two ship as Favorites: Tech and Camera Kit.

    Eleven never appear in the picker at all because every trip gets them automatically — Travel Prep, Home, Evening Preparations, On You, Departure Sequence, Unpacking, What Didn't Work, What Worked Well, For Next Time, Activities, Notes to Self.

    Section or kit?

    The setting is a two-way switch at the top of the editor, and it changes one thing: whether items inside are shared with the rest of the trip.

    A section shares. Put a driver's licence in Travel Documents and On You and it's one licence. Both rows say "Also in …", ticking one ticks the other, editing one edits both.

    A kit doesn't. Sunscreen in the Beach Kit and sunscreen in the Hiking Gear kit are two bottles. They tick separately and you pack both, which is correct — they live in different bags.

    Rule of thumb: if you'd physically pick the whole thing up and put it in the car, it's a kit.

    Priority: what gets offered when

    Set in the section editor, three options:

    PriorityWhat it doesWhere it shows
    Essential ⭐Pre-ticked on every new tripTop of Add Sections, already selected
    Favorite ♥Your personal go-tosSecond group, easy to reach
    No PriorityThe defaultOffered under Other Sections / Other Kits when relevant

    If you find yourself adding the same kit to every trip, make it Essential and stop deciding.

    Tags: when a section is relevant

    Every section can carry tags across five axes — trip type, weather, season, transportation, duration. They control which sections get suggested for a trip.

    Leaving a tag empty means "always relevant." Not "never" — empty is the permissive setting.

    Two behaviours worth knowing, because they're deliberately different:

    • A section is never hidden by its tags. A non-matching section drops to the "other" groups but stays available. You can always add anything.
    • An item can be hidden by its tags. A passport only materialises on a trip tagged International; a mail-hold task only on a long trip. Items are opt-in.

    Transportation is the one exception that can genuinely exclude a section from the suggested groups — Car Essentials on a flight, for instance.

    Making your own

    Settings → Sections & Kits Library → +

    • Name, emoji, colour, description
    • Section or Kit
    • Priority
    • Relevance tags
    • Items, added inline — type a name and either pick a library match (which adds it by reference, no duplicate created) or take the Create "…" option to make a brand-new item

    Items are organised per phase inside the editor, so you can decide that your "Race Day" kit puts some things on Prep and others on Pack.

    You can also make a section for one trip only: Manage Sections → Custom Section. It lives in that trip and never enters your library.

    Browsing and editing kits

    Sections & Kits Library → Browse Kits — described in the app as "Add activity and specialty gear" — shows each kit with everything inside it and lets you set per-kit defaults.

    That last part is the useful bit: the same item can carry different defaults in different kits. Sunscreen might be ×1 in the Beach Kit and ×2 in Ski Gear, with a different note on each. Those overrides live on the kit, not on the item, so they don't leak anywhere else. A customised entry is labelled "Customized for [kit name]".

    Go deeper

    Filtering the library. The library's filter covers Sections, Kits, Favorites, Essentials, No Priority, and which phase a list feeds (Prep / Pack / Travel / Return), plus a search field and Clear All.

    Archiving. Open a section → Archive. It stops being offered on new trips and moves to Archived Sections & Kits at the bottom of the library. Nothing is lost and existing trips are untouched. This is the right move for "I'll never use Golf Gear" — better than deleting, which is permanent.

    Groups inside a section. Items can be grouped (Tops, Bottoms, Shoes), and groups are what make a big section readable, since they arrive collapsed. Add groups as you add items in the editor.

    Deleting a library section is safe for your trips. Every trip keeps its own copy of everything. Deleting Golf Gear from the library does not alter, break, or empty a trip that used it.

    If something looks wrong

    I added a section to my trip and it was empty. Sections show items only on the phases those items belong to. A section whose items all live on Pack looks empty from Prep.

    An item I expected didn't come across. It was probably filtered out by your trip's tags. Add it by hand with the +, or loosen the item's tags in the Item Library so it applies more widely.

    I edited a section and my current trip didn't change. Expected. Library edits reach the next trip you make. See How the app is organized.

    Two copies of the same thing keep appearing. They're in two kits, which don't share by design. If they should be one thing, move them into sections instead.

    I can't find a section I made. Check the library's filter — if Kits is selected and you made a section, it won't be listed. Then check Archived.
