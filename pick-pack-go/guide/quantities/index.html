    Quantity formulas

    Stop counting socks.

    The gist

    • An item can work out its own quantity from your trip's length. Six rules to choose from.
    • Underwear and socks are "Days + 2". Shirts are "Days ÷ 2". That's most of what the library uses them for.
    • The number is worked out when the trip is created — and then it's yours to override.
    • Change your dates later and the per-day numbers recount — the app asks first, and never touches a number you set by hand.
    • Set formulas in the Item Library. On a trip you just set a number.

    The six rules

    RuleMathsOn a 2-night tripOn a 5-night tripOn a 14-night trip

    "Days" means nights away — the gap between your departure and return dates. Both halving rules have a floor, so a one-night trip never tells you to bring zero shirts.

    What the library actually does with them

    Worth knowing, because it explains why most items just say 1:

    RuleUsed byWhy
    Days + 2Underwear, socks, thongsThe things you want spares of. Two extra covers a spill and a delay
    Days ÷ 2 (round up)T-shirts, tank tops, camis, tightsWorn against the skin, so roughly every other day, minimum two
    Days ÷ 2 (round down)Shirts, blouses, sweaters, jeans, trousers, skirts, shorts, dresses — 32 garmentsRe-wearable. Nobody needs seven pairs of jeans for a week
    Always 2A handful of specifics
    Always 1Everything else — the large majority of the 839A hairbrush is a hairbrush

    So the formulas are doing their work almost entirely inside Clothes, which is exactly where counting is annoying and where over-packing happens.

    Setting one

    Settings → Item Library → the item → Default Quantity. Pick a rule; choose Always Manual and a stepper appears for your own number.

    Per kit, you can override the default: Sections & Kits Library → Browse Kits → the kit → the item. The same item can be ×1 in one kit and ×2 in another, and the override lives on the kit, so it doesn't leak. A customised entry shows "Customized for [kit name]".

    On a trip, you just set a number

    There is no formula picker inside a trip — and that's deliberate. Once the trip exists, the maths has already happened; what you want at that point is "make it four".

    • Tap the ×3 badge on a row to edit the number in place.
    • Press and hold → Change Quantity, or open the editor and use the stepper.

    Setting a number by hand replaces the calculated one for that trip only. Your library rule is untouched.

    Go deeper

    Templates and length. A template has a nominal length in nights. When you make a trip from it, quantities are worked out against your trip's dates, not the template's — so the same template gives you three shirts for a weekend and seven for a fortnight. The template's own number matters only when nothing else is available to count against.

    Changing dates recounts — after asking. Quantities are worked out when the trip is created and stored on the trip. If you move your dates afterwards, the app recalculates the per-day ones and shows you what it intends to change before it changes anything.

    Three kinds of row it never touches:

    1. Numbers you set by hand — anything you typed, and anything on Always Manual.
    2. Items you added yourself.
    3. Rows the same change is removing — moving your dates can also change which items belong.

    The alert tells you how many quantities are changing; tap Review Updates to see which ones. A real date edit can produce twenty-odd changes, which is why it counts rather than listing them.

    This is newer behaviour. Before it existed, stretching a weekend into a fortnight added the fortnight's items but left every quantity at the weekend's figure, silently — an under-pack, which is the exact thing this app exists to prevent.

    Plural names come along for the ride. The library stores both "Sock" and "Socks" so a calculated quantity reads properly.

    If something looks wrong

    Everything says 1. That's the library default and it's correct for most objects. Only clothing ships with per-day rules. If you want more things counted per-day, set their rules in the Item Library.

    My quantities changed when I moved the dates. That's intended, and the app asked before doing it. Anything you'd set by hand was left exactly as you left it.

    Some quantities changed and others didn't. The ones that moved are on a per-day rule. The ones that didn't are either fixed (Always 1, Always 2), set by hand, or rows you added yourself.

    My item shows no number at all. Only Items display a quantity. To-Dos, Errands and Notes never show one, even if a number was stored. Prep, Plans and Return create new entries as To-Dos by default, which is the usual reason a typed "3 chargers" shows no 3.

    A weekend trip gave me two T-shirts when the maths says one. The round-up rule has a floor of two. That's intentional.

    I set a quantity on the trip and my library changed too. It didn't — check the library and you'll find the original rule intact. Trip edits never write back.
