    Packing day

    You're at the bag. Here's what every gesture does.

    The gist

    • Tap the circle to check something off. It shows as done for about a second, then gets out of your way.
    • Your phone won't lock while you're on a packing list. Put it down, cross the room, come back.
    • Some items appear twice on purpose — checking one checks the other. The eyebrow "Also in …" tells you when.
    • Swipe left to delete, swipe right to flag as critical (or the reverse — it's a setting).
    • Press and hold any row for everything else: quantity, edit, critical, charged, delete.

    The gestures

    You do thisYou get
    Tap the circleChecks the item off
    Tap the ×3 badgeEdit just the number, in place
    Double-tap the rowOpens the full editor
    Swipe one wayDelete (no confirmation)
    Swipe the other wayMark Critical and Edit
    Press and holdMenu: Change Quantity · Edit · Mark Critical · Mark Charged · Delete Item
    Drag a rowMove it — anywhere within its own section
    Tap a group's rowCollapse or expand it
    Press and hold a group's rowRename Group
    Swipe a group's rowDelete All or Ungroup

    Swipe direction is yours. Settings → Actions → Swipe to Delete puts delete on the left or the right. Everything else follows.

    Dragging is always available. There's no reorder mode to enter and no mode to escape — press, hold briefly, and move. If you hold long enough for the menu to appear, keep moving anyway and the menu turns into the drag.

    What happens when you check something off

    The row shows its tick and strikethrough for about a second, then slides away — because Hide Completed is the default. That pause is deliberate: it's long enough to see you got the right one, short enough to keep the list shrinking.

    Section pills count down as you go — "7 items left" becomes "6 items left", then "✓ Complete!".

    A phase-wide count appears under the title once you're underway: "24 items remaining to pack". It's hidden before you've checked anything and hidden again when you're done, so it only shows while there's something to measure.

    When everything in the phase is checked, you get a completion notice instead of an empty screen.

    Want to see your progress instead of watching it vanish? ••• → Show Completed. Checked items stay put, struck through, and collect under a COMPLETED heading at the bottom of each section.

    The same thing in two places

    Your driver's licence is in Wallet & Docs and in On You. That's not a bug and it's not a duplicate — it's the same object appearing in the two lists where you'd look for it.

    Each copy carries a small line above the name: Also in On You. And because they're linked:

    • Checking one checks the other. You can't accidentally pack it twice.
    • Marking one as charged marks the other.
    • Editing one edits both.
    • Deleting one deletes only that copy — the other stays.

    Items that came from kits deliberately don't link. Sunscreen in your Beach Kit and sunscreen in your Hiking Kit are two separate bottles as far as the app is concerned, and you tick them separately.

    Go deeper

    Flag the things that would ruin the trip. Swipe right (or hold → Mark Critical) on your passport, your medication, the charger for the thing that can't be replaced. Critical items get a red marker — and every one of them is gathered automatically into the Double Check panel on the Travel tab for one final pass before you leave. See Travel day and the Double Check.

    Filter to just what needs charging. ••• → Filter → Items to Charge the night before, then work that short list. Marking something charged also marks its linked copies.

    Reorder to match your actual route. Drag sections in Manage Sections, and items within a section directly on the list, so the order matches how you physically move through your home. The app doesn't know your flat; you do.

    Group things yourself. Press and hold a group heading to rename it. Two groups can share a name if that's genuinely what you want.

    If something looks wrong

    I checked the wrong thing and it disappeared. ••• → Show Completed to bring checked items back into view, find it, uncheck it, then turn Show Completed off again. There's no undo.

    A whole section vanished when I checked its last item. It moved into the COMPLETED SECTIONS row at the bottom. Tap that to see it. Nothing was deleted.

    The list moved under my finger. Checking the last item in a section makes the section collapse away, which reflows everything below. If this keeps catching you out, turn on Show Completed — nothing moves then.

    I can't drag an item into a different section. That's a deliberate limit — drags are clamped to the section they start in, and you'll see the "no drop" badge if you wander out. Open the item's editor and change its Section there instead.

    A clothing row says worn 3 times under its name. That garment is in three of the outfits you planned on the Plans tab. It's still one thing to pack — packing it once covers all three days. See Planning outfits.
