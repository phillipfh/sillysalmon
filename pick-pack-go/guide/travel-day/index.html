    Travel day and the Double Check

    The last look before the door closes.

    The gist

    • The Travel tab ends with a Double Check panel — right at the bottom, below everything else. You don't have to build it, find it, or turn it on.
    • It gathers every Critical item from your Prep and Pack lists into one short list.
    • Ticking it there is a separate confirmation from packing it. Packed means "it's in the bag"; double-checked means "I have looked at it with my own eyes."
    • It's last on purpose. You work the list top to bottom, and this is the final gate before the door — so it's the last thing you meet.
    • Above it sit On You and Departure Sequence — what's in your pockets, and the last sweep of the house.

    What Double Check is

    Somewhere in a hundred-and-something item list are the six things that would actually ruin the trip: passport, medication, the charger for the thing you can't replace, the keys.

    Every item you've marked Critical — plus the ones the library already ships as critical — is collected into a single panel pinned to the bottom of the Travel tab, below every section. Nothing to set up. It appears because you're on Travel and you have critical items.

    It sits at the bottom on purpose. You work a list top to bottom, and this is the last gate before the door — so it's the last thing you meet. Its position is fixed: it isn't a section you can drag, and it stays put whether or not you have completed items hidden away above it.

    The panel explains itself in place:

    Items marked ⚠ Critical on your Prep and Pack lists will appear here for a double check.

    Do it

    1. Open the Travel tab. On the day you're leaving, the app opens here by itself.

    2. Work down the Double Check panel. For each row, physically look at the thing. Then tick it.

    3. Note that this tick is not the packing tick. An item can be packed but not double-checked, or double-checked but not ticked as packed. They're separate on purpose — the whole point is a second, independent pass, and reusing the same tick would make it a formality.

    4. Then do On You — wallet, phone, keys, glasses — and Departure Sequence, the last sweep of the house.

    Marking things critical

    Anything can be critical. Three ways to flag one:

    • Swipe the row (the direction that isn't delete) → Mark Critical
    • Press and hold the row → Mark Critical
    • Open the item's editor and turn on the critical setting

    Critical items show a red marker on the list, can be filtered to on their own (••• → Filter → Critical Items), and appear in Double Check.

    Make something critical in your library and every future trip inherits it — Settings → Item Library → the item → mark it critical there. See The Item Library.

    Go deeper

    The same item can appear twice in Double Check, on purpose. If something is critical on both your Prep list and your Pack list, you get a row for each — they're different moments, and the app won't merge them.

    Kit items each get their own row. Sunscreen from a Beach Kit and sunscreen from a Hiking Kit are two bottles, so they're two rows. Items that came from ordinary sections and are genuinely the same object collapse into one row.

    You can turn the panel off. Settings → Actions → Auto-add critical items to Double Check section. It's on by default. With it off, the Travel tab is just your Travel list.

    If something looks wrong

    A checklist item told me to "Filter by Critical Items in the upper right ☝️". That note is out of date — there's no Critical control in the upper right. It's in ••• → Filter → Critical Items.

    More usefully: you don't need to do it at all. The Double Check panel on the Travel tab has already gathered exactly the items that note is asking you to go and find. Tick the note off and use the panel.

    The Double Check panel isn't there. Three possibilities: you're not on the Travel tab; nothing on this trip is marked Critical yet; or the setting above is turned off.

    I ticked something in Double Check and it didn't check off on the Pack list. Correct — they're independent. Double Check confirms you've seen it, not that you've packed it.

    The Travel tab looks wrong for what I'm doing. If you're at the far end of the trip and packing to go home, you want the reset — see Coming home.
