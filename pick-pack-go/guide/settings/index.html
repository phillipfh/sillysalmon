    Settings

    Every switch, in plain language.

    The gist

    • Settings is the gear at the top left of Home.
    • Libraries is the important part — templates, sections and kits, items.
    • Actions holds three behaviour switches, one of which only affects trips you haven't made yet.
    • Appearance does font, text size and light/dark.
    • Reset Options is destructive and lives behind its own screen.

    Reset Options

    Behind its own screen, described as "Manage destructive actions like clearing data or resetting to defaults." Two actions:

    ActionWhat it does
    Reset Libraries to DefaultsPuts the sections, kits and items back the way they shipped. Your own additions and edits to the library are lost. Your trips are not touched
    Delete All Trip HistoryDeletes your trips. Your libraries and templates are not touched

    Both ask for confirmation. Neither can be undone, and there's no export — so treat them as final.

    One warning about that screen's warning. The grey note under both buttons reads "This will permanently delete all past, current, and upcoming trips and their packing lists." That's only true of Delete All Trip History. Resetting your libraries does not delete trips — your trips keep their own copy of everything and come through untouched. Go by the confirmation dialog, which is accurate for each action.

    Go deeper

    What isn't in Settings, and why.

    • No account or sign-in. There's nothing to log into.
    • No sync settings. Your data lives on this iPhone, and is included in its iCloud device backup. Restoring a new phone from backup brings everything. Two phones will not stay in step.
    • No notification settings. The app never notifies you about anything.
    • No export or backup button. Your only copy is your device backup. Worth knowing before you use Reset Options.

    If something looks wrong

    I'm looking for the "Plan clothes by outfit" switch. It's gone. Outfit planning moved to the Plans tab and needs nothing switched on — every trip has an Outfits block. See Planning outfits.

    I changed Text Size and a sheet didn't follow. It should — the setting applies at the window level, so sheets, menus and lists all inherit it. If something looks unchanged, close and reopen it.

    I reset my libraries and lost my custom sections. That's what "reset to defaults" means, and it can't be undone. Your trips and templates survive — and since templates carry their own copies of everything, a template you'd saved still holds those custom sections.

    Where's the setting for _____? If it isn't on that screen, it probably isn't in the app. See the list above.
