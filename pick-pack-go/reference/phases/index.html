    Five phases, four tabs

    The trip timeline, the tab that swaps, and where each phase's items come from.

    Checked against the app on 2026-09-18.

    PhaseWhat it holdsWhen it's for
    Preperrands and tasks before you touch a bagdays before
    Packthe suitcase itself — physical objectsthe night before
    Travelwhat happens on the day you leavedeparture day
    Plansoutfits, activities, notes for while you're thereduring
    Returngetting home, and what you learnedarrival onward

    Five exist; four are visible

    Every trip auto-creates all five PackingList records. The tab bar shows four at a time.

    Prep and Return trade places at one date boundary — the day after arrival. Before it, Prep is in the bar and Return isn't; after it, they swap. A Phases submenu in the ••• menu reaches all five at any time, so nothing is ever unreachable.

    The detached circle beside the tabs is the magic +. It's declared as a fifth, search-role tab, which is why the bar holds four content tabs rather than five.

    Why a timeline instead of one list

    "Pack for the trip" is a single undifferentiated task with no natural entry point. Splitting it into five gives each part a moment when it becomes relevant, so you're never looking at the passport-check item while you're deciding how many shirts to bring.

    The split also means the trip home is modelled rather than assumed. Return is a real phase with its own items, not an afterthought once you're already away.

    What lands where

    Seeded items carry the phase they belong to. Three phases default new entries to To-Do rather than Item:

    PhaseDefault type for new rows
    PrepTo-Do
    PackItem
    TravelItem
    PlansTo-Do
    ReturnTo-Do

    This is the usual reason a typed "3 chargers" shows no quantity — only Items display one. To-Dos, Errands and Notes never do, even if a number was stored.

    Double Check

    A computed section on Travel. Not stored, not editable as a section.

    It is pinned to the bottom, below every section including the completed group — not the top. That is deliberate: "People work a list top to bottom, and this is the last gate before the door — so it reads last."

    Its position is fixed by construction. It is its own collection-view section rather than rows appended to the last item section, so reordering sections cannot move it, and it stays put whether or not the COMPLETED group is present.

    It led the list in an earlier build, which is why some older descriptions put it first. Checked on device, 2026-09-18.

    It gathers every item flagged Critical from Prep and Pack, and gives each its own confirmation tick — independent of whether the item is packed. Ticking "packed" at 11pm and confirming at the door are different cognitive events, and the app treats them as different acts.

    33 items ship Critical-flagged; any item can be marked Critical.

    How a critical item looks: its checkbox is replaced by a red exclamationmark.circle. Ordinary rows show a plain grey hollow circle. The warning occupies the control you tap, rather than sitting beside it. On You rows are deliberately excluded from this treatment and keep plain circles, so two similar confirm controls never share a screen.
