The 30-second version 🧳

5 steps
  • Tap + New Trip, type where you're going, tap a few pills, tap Next, then Go Pack.
  • You get a full list. Cut it down: swipe items left to remove them, or remove whole sections in Manage Sections.
  • Tap circles to tick things off. Press and hold anything to change it.
  • Check the other tabs: Prep for errands, Travel for the day you leave.
  • When the list fits you, ••• → Save as Template so next time starts there.

Tap a circle to tick a step off, the same way you will in the app.

The idea in one minute 💡

1 screen

Most packing apps hand you an empty list and a search box. That's the hard way round. A blank page is exactly where planning stalls, especially for a brain that finds starting the hardest part.

Pick, Pack, Go does the opposite. Tell it roughly what kind of trip this is, and it hands you a list that's already too long. Then you trim it. Saying “don't need that” to hiking boots takes a second. Remembering sunscreen out of nowhere at 11pm takes a lot more.

  • Your list starts full.Every trip arrives with a real packing list, built from a library of hundreds of items. You never start from nothing.
  • Your job is crossing off.When the list feels long, that's normal. You're meant to cut it down, not fill it in.
  • Packing is five small jobs.Prep, Pack, Travel, Plans and Return each get their own tab, so you only see today's job.
  • Stray taps can't hurt.Anything that changes your list sits behind a swipe or a press and hold. A normal tap only ticks things off.
  • Experiment freely.What you change on a trip stays on that trip. Your library only changes when you change it on purpose.
  • No account.Everything lives on your iPhone and comes along in your normal iCloud backup.

The first screen says it too

  1. 1Pick sections, not items. You choose whole categories, like Toiletries or Tech, and the items come with them.
  2. 2Everything's already in here. The library is built for you. Make it yours over time.
  3. 3Designed for focus. Edits are a press and hold, a swipe or a double tap away, so nothing pulls you off your list by accident.
  4. 4Tap Set Up in 4 Seconds to start.

First launch 👋

1 screen

One question, then you're in. There's nothing to sign up for.

What do you pack?

  1. 1Clothes. Tick the styles you wear. Tick both if you like.
  2. 2Also. Makeup and Period Care are optional extras.
  3. 3Continue. Anything you leave unticked is put away, not deleted. You can bring it back any time.
  4. 4Skip for Now works too. It changes nothing.

Good to know

This screen only removes things you'd never pack, so you're not scrolling past them later.

Make your first trip 🧳

2 screens

On Home, tap + New Trip in the top right. It takes about thirty seconds.

The New Trip sheet

  1. 1Type where you're going.
  2. 2The emoji picks itself. Lisbon became a tram. Tap it to choose your own.
  3. 3Dates. New trips start tomorrow for three nights. The dates decide how many socks and shirts you're told to bring.
  4. 4Color. Optional. The whole trip takes it on.
  5. 5Tap what fits: how you're getting there, the weather, the kind of trip. These decide which items show up. A passport only appears if you tap International.
  6. 6Next.

Good to know

A wrong pill isn't a problem. You can change any of this later from the trip's ••• menu.

Add Sections

  1. 1Essentials are already ticked. Clothes, Personal Items, Toiletries, Travel Essentials, Wallet & Docs.
  2. 2Tap a row to add it. Tech is a favorite, so it's offered near the top. Added rows go gray and say “Added!”
  3. 3Suggested Sections match the pills you tapped on the last screen.
  4. 4Other Sections and Other Kits hold everything else. Scroll down for them.
  5. 5Go Pack builds the trip.

Good to know

Don't agonize here. Adding or removing a section later takes two taps.

Your packing list ✅

2 screens

This is where you'll spend most of your time. The trip opens on whichever tab fits today's date, so a trip tomorrow opens on Pack.

How the list is laid out

  1. 1A section. The colored bar, with how many items are left in it.
  2. 2Groups like Tops and Shoes start folded shut, with a count. Clothes is 43 items, but folded it's eight rows. Tap one to open it.
  3. 3An item. Tap its circle to tick it off.
  4. 4Manage Sections. Add, remove and reorder whole sections. More on this below.
  5. 5••• holds trip options: show ticked items, filter, edit the trip, save it as a template.
  6. 6The tabs. Each phase of the trip has its own list.
  7. 7+ adds something wherever you are.

Ticking things off

  1. 1Tap the circle. The item is crossed out.
  2. 2The count goes down for the whole trip and for each section.
  3. 3× 2 means bring two. The app works quantities out from the length of the trip.
  4. 4Group counts go down too.

Good to know

Ticked items tuck themselves away a moment after your last tap, so the list gets shorter as you go and never jumps under your thumb. Turn on Show Completed in ••• to see them again.

Cut the list down ✂️

3 screens

The list starts long on purpose. Trimming it is the main skill, and none of it touches your library. Next trip starts fresh.

Remove one item

  1. 1Swipe an item to the left and tap Remove. No “are you sure?”. At 11pm you want speed.

Manage Sections: the big cut

  1. 1Red minus takes a whole section off this trip. One tap can clear twenty items.
  2. 2Drag the handles to reorder. Put what you pack first at the top.
  3. 3It works on the tab you're on. This one says Manage Pack Sections. Open it from Prep and it manages Prep.
  4. 4Custom Section makes a new one of your own. See the next chapter.
  5. 5Other Sections in this Trip live on other tabs. Tap the green plus to show one here too.

Good to know

Open this with the Manage Sections button at the top of any list, just left of •••.

Add a section you skipped

  1. 1Available Sections come from your library. Tap the green plus and the section arrives with all its items.
  2. 2Available Kits are bags of gear you grab as a unit, like a Gifts kit or a Camera Kit.

Add your own section 🤿

4 screens

Six related things you thought of? Give them a section. Here's a Snorkel Gear section made from scratch.

Manage Sections → Custom Section

  1. 1Name it.
  2. 2Tap the emoji to change it. Search Emoji finds things by name, like “diving mask”.
  3. 3Pick a color.
  4. 4Tap the check to add it.

It joins the trip

  1. 1Created for this trip means it lives on this trip only, for now. Drag it wherever you want it.
  2. 2Done takes you back to the list.

Fill it up

  1. 1Tap + Add Item under the section, type a name, press Return. Snorkel and mask, Fins, Rash guard.
  2. 2The field stays open after each one, so you can dump five things in a row without tapping again.
  3. 3Tap ✕ when you're done.

It suggests as you type

  1. 1Start typing. Here, “Sun”.
  2. 2Create “Sun” makes a brand-new item. Return does the same.
  3. 3In this trip finds things already on the list elsewhere. Pick Sunscreen and it's the same bottle in both places, so ticking one ticks the other.

Good to know

The big + at the bottom right does the same thing from anywhere. Press and drag it instead of tapping to drop the new item exactly where you want it.

Change a section or an item ✏️

2 screens

Edits live behind a press and hold. That's deliberate: a normal tap should only ever tick something off.

Press and hold a section

  1. 1In Manage Sections, press and hold the section.
  2. 2Edit Section lets you rename it and change its emoji or color.
  3. 3Add to Library keeps it for every future trip. Otherwise it stays on this trip only.
  4. 4A peek inside shows what's in it, which helps before you remove a section.

Good to know

Edit Section and Add to Library appear on sections you made for the trip. To change a library section for every trip, see the next chapter.

Press and hold an item

  1. 1Press and hold any item.
  2. 2Mark Critical for the things that would ruin the trip. They get a second check on travel day.
  3. 3Add to Group files it under a heading, like Tops.
  4. 4Change Quantity.
  5. 5Edit Item for its name, notes, type and section.
  6. 6Save to Item Library so future trips offer it too.
  7. 7Remove from Trip.

Sections for every future trip 📚

4 screens

Everything so far changed one trip. To change what every new trip starts with, use the libraries in Settings. Tap the sliders button at the top left of Home.

Settings

  1. 1Template Library. Whole trip setups you reuse.
  2. 2Sections & Kits Library. The lists every trip is built from.
  3. 3Item Library. Every item, with its quantity rules and notes.

Sections & Kits Library

  1. 1+ makes a new section or kit for your library.
  2. 2Browse Kits has more ready-made kits: hiking, ski, beach, baby gear and more.
  3. 3Tap any section to edit it.
  4. 4Search when the list gets long.

Editing a library section

  1. 1Section or Kit. See the box below.
  2. 2Name, emoji, description and color.
  3. 3Priority. Essential sections come pre-ticked on every new trip. Favorites are offered near the top.
  4. 4Suggested for… decides which trips this section is offered for.
  5. 5Items, sorted by tab. A section can put some things on Prep and others on Pack. Tap + Add Item under any tab.

Suggested for…

  1. 1Tap the trips this section suits: transport, weather, trip type, length.
  2. 2The summary says what you've set in plain words. You can still add any section to any trip by hand.

Section or kit?

Sections share. Your passport in Wallet & Docs and in On You is one passport, shown in two places, so ticking it once ticks both. Kits don't share. Sunscreen in your Beach Kit and your Hiking Kit is two bottles, because they live in two bags. Rule of thumb: if you'd pick the whole thing up and put it in the car, make it a kit.

Library or trip?

Think of the library as a cookbook and a trip as a meal you cooked from it. Changing the recipe doesn't change last week's dinner. Library changes show up in the next trip you make. Trip changes stay on that trip.

Prep: three or more days out

  1. 1Travel Prep is errands with lead time.
  2. 2To-dos, not things to pack. Notify your card, get cash, check your passport's expiry.
  3. 3Kits bring their own prep. The Camera Kit adds “Format memory cards” here.
  4. 4The Prep tab.

Travel: the day you leave

  1. 1On You is what's in your pockets.
  2. 2(say out loud) Saying “passport” out loud as you touch it makes it stick.
  3. 3Home is the last walk through the house: trash, windows, locks.
  4. 4The Travel tab.

Travel: the Double Check

  1. 1Double Check sits at the very bottom of Travel. It gathers every item marked Critical into one short list.
  2. 2The red marks are confirm buttons. Packed means it's in the bag. Confirmed means you've looked at it with your own eyes.

Good to know

Nothing to set up. Mark an item Critical (press and hold it) and it shows up here.

Plans: while you're there

  1. 1Outfits. Plan what you'll wear, if that's the part you get stuck on.
  2. 2Day pills switch between days of the trip.
  3. 3Garments go onto your packing list by themselves. The label under White T-shirt says it's also in Tuesday's outfit.
  4. 4Activities and Notes to Self for the trip itself.

Good to know

Nothing on Plans counts toward your progress. Ticking things here is just for you.

Next time is faster 🔁

3 screens

Once a trip fits how you actually travel, keep it.

The ••• menu

  1. 1Save as Template. The trimmed list becomes the starting point next time. This is the biggest time saver in the app.
  2. 2Pack for Return un-ticks what needs to come home, so you can pack the same list again in the hotel room.
  3. 3Edit Trip Details for name, dates and pills.
  4. 4Filter to just critical items, things to charge, errands or to-dos.
  5. 5Show Completed brings ticked items back into view.

Templates

  1. 1Start a trip from a template and skip choosing sections.
  2. 2+ builds a new one from scratch.

Good to know

Open this from the stacked-cards button on Home, next to + New Trip, then All Templates. Heart a template to put it in that menu.

Home, once you've got trips

  1. 1+ New Trip.
  2. 2Templates.
  3. 3Settings and the libraries.
  4. 4Current Trips show how soon each one is. Upcoming trips sit below.
  5. 5View Past Trips. Finished trips are kept, so you can copy one and start with everything you changed last time.

What it doesn't do

  • It never notifies you.No reminders or countdowns. Set an alarm the way you normally would.
  • It doesn't sync between devices.Your data is on this iPhone and in its iCloud backup, so a new phone restored from backup gets everything.
  • It doesn't share lists.No collaborators, for now.
  • It doesn't check the weather.You tell it whether the trip is hot or cold.
  • iPhone only.No iPad or web version yet.

If the list ever feels like too much: fold the groups, open Manage Sections, and take off anything you don't need. That's the app working as intended.
